COMMON QUESTIONS

About the workbook.

Is this a live web application?

No. The Margin Tracker is a downloadable spreadsheet workbook. You enter and maintain your own business data; it is not connected to your booking, accounting or payment systems.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for clinic owners reviewing service economics and operating figures. If you run a salon, barber, beauty, tattoo or other service business, contact Risenup to discuss whether the workbook is a fit or whether a tailored tool would be more suitable.

Does it replace an accountant or bookkeeper?

No. It is a planning and organisation tool, not accounting, tax, payroll or financial advice. Check calculations and business decisions with the qualified adviser who understands your circumstances.

What is the current price and what are the purchase terms?

The secure checkout shows the current price, delivery details and terms before you pay. Review those details there before purchasing.

Can Risenup customise tools for my business?

Yes. Custom tools, integrations and business improvement plans are handled through a separate consulting engagement. The scope, access requirements, deliverables and fee are agreed before work starts.

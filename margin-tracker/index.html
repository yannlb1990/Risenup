COMMON QUESTIONS

About the workbook.

Is this a software app or a spreadsheet?

It is a downloadable Excel workbook, not a live app. You enter and update your own figures; it does not connect to your booking, payment or accounting systems.

Who is the workbook designed for?

It is designed for clinics, especially skin, aesthetic and beauty practices that want to review treatments, retail products and operating costs. Other service businesses can request a discovery call to discuss whether it fits.

What can I track in it?

The workbook includes daily sales, treatment and package inputs, product margins and stock, overheads, marketing records and weekly, monthly and yearly summaries.

Do I need to complete every sheet?

No. Start with the sheets that match the information you have and the question you want to review. You can build on it as your record-keeping routine develops.

Does it replace an accountant or bookkeeper?

No. It is a business-planning workbook, not accounting, tax, payroll or financial advice. Check tax and employment decisions with a qualified adviser who understands your circumstances.

What does the secure checkout show?

Stripe displays the current price, delivery details and purchase terms before payment. Review those details there before you complete your purchase.

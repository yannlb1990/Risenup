WHY THIS CHOICE MATTERS

Start with the work, then choose the tool.

Specialist support works best when the business knows the decision it needs help with and what evidence the professional should review. Clear scope prevents gaps between business operations and regulated advice.

01 · CHARACTERISTICS

What it should handle

A defined service scope, responsibilities, information request, advice format and follow-up arrangement.

02 · ADVANTAGE

What a good fit can improve

More focused conversations and clearer accountability for what is included and what must be handled by another professional.

03 · PROOF OF FIT

How to check before committing

Ask for relevant experience, registration or qualifications where applicable, professional indemnity cover, written scope, fees and how advice will be delivered.

PRACTICAL SELECTION CHECKLIST

Questions to ask before you decide.

  • Write down the decision or recurring task you need help with.
  • Check relevant credentials and professional registers for the service requested.
  • Ask what is included, excluded, due when, and who owns each action.
  • Understand fee basis, additional charges, file access and handover arrangements.
  • Share only the information the adviser needs, using an agreed secure method.
  • Record advice, assumptions and next steps, and keep operational consulting separate from regulated advice.
Keep the decision measurable. Record what matters today, what needs to work better, the full cost and who will own the system. Compare providers against the same checklist, then test a realistic workflow before rollout.

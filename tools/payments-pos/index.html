WHY THIS CHOICE MATTERS

Start with the work, then choose the tool.

A payment setup affects the checkout experience and how cleanly sales reconcile. The useful choice is the one that fits your service flow, cash handling and accounting process, with full costs and responsibilities understood.

01 · CHARACTERISTICS

What it should handle

Payment acceptance, deposits, refunds, receipts and sales reporting in one connected flow.

02 · ADVANTAGE

What a good fit can improve

Fewer manual hand-offs at checkout and a clearer path from service sale to reconciliation, when the tools fit your process.

03 · PROOF OF FIT

How to check before committing

Run a realistic test sale, refund and end-of-day reconciliation. Confirm settlement timing and all fees using the provider’s current Australian terms.

PRACTICAL SELECTION CHECKLIST

Questions to ask before you decide.

  • List the payment types and sales channels you use.
  • Compare the full transaction and monthly cost, including terminal rental, chargeback and refund treatment.
  • Check settlement timing, payout reports, refund handling and reconciliation exports.
  • Confirm compatibility with booking and accounting software before signing.
  • Test ordinary sales, split payments, deposits, cancellations and refunds.
  • Agree who handles disputes, outages and customer questions.
Keep the decision measurable. Record what matters today, what needs to work better, the full cost and who will own the system. Compare providers against the same checklist, then test a realistic workflow before rollout.

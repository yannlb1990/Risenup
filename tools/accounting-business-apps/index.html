WHY THIS CHOICE MATTERS

Start with the work, then choose the tool.

Accounting and operations tools should support a dependable flow of information from sale to bank reconciliation and adviser review. The business keeps responsibility for accurate records and should understand what each system does.

FEATURES TO CHECK

What it should handle

Invoices, expenses, bank feeds, payroll links, sales summaries, access controls and exports.

BUSINESS ADVANTAGE

What a good fit can improve

Less repeated entry and better-organised records when each system is configured for the actual workflow.

PROOF OF FIT

How to check before committing

Use a sample sales period to compare the point-of-sale summary, payment payout and accounting entries. Ask your bookkeeper or accountant to review the setup before relying on reports.

PRACTICAL SELECTION CHECKLIST

Questions to ask before you decide.

  • List the records you need and who prepares, reviews and approves them.
  • Check Australian tax and payroll features against your adviser’s requirements.
  • Confirm data import, export, backup and access when staff or advisers change.
  • Map the path from booking or POS through payment provider to accounting ledger.
  • Compare subscription tiers, user limits, add-ons, support and migration costs.
  • Test reconciliation and reporting with sample data before changing live processes.
Keep the decision measurable. Record what matters today, what needs to work better, the full cost and who will own the system. Compare providers against the same checklist, then test a realistic workflow before rollout.

WHY THIS CHOICE MATTERS

Start with the work, then choose the tool.

Small businesses depend on bookings, payments, client records, internet access and trusted suppliers. A few documented routines can make it easier to spot problems, limit disruption and recover carefully.

01 · CHARACTERISTICS

What it should handle

Account access, updates, backups, staff roles, incident response and service-continuity steps.

02 · ADVANTAGE

What a good fit can improve

A team that knows who to contact, what to protect and how to keep essential work safe during a disruption.

03 · PROOF OF FIT

How to check before committing

Check a backup can actually be restored; review user access; walk through a lost-device or system-outage scenario and record gaps without exposing sensitive data.

PRACTICAL SELECTION CHECKLIST

Questions to ask before you decide.

  • Enable multi-factor authentication on key accounts and remove unused access.
  • Use unique credentials and a password manager; define an authorised recovery contact.
  • Back up important records independently and test restoration on a safe sample.
  • Know provider, bank, insurer and specialist incident contact routes.
  • Write down safe fallback steps for bookings, customer messages and payment disruptions.
  • Review privacy and notification duties with a qualified adviser when an incident occurs.
Keep the decision measurable. Record what matters today, what needs to work better, the full cost and who will own the system. Compare providers against the same checklist, then test a realistic workflow before rollout.

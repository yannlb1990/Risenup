WHY THIS CHOICE MATTERS

Start with the work, then choose the tool.

Booking software shapes how clients find a service, choose a time, receive reminders, pay a deposit and return. A clear workflow can reduce missed hand-offs and make the client journey easier to manage.

FEATURES TO CHECK

What it should handle

Service catalogue, appointment slots, reminders, deposits, staff calendars, client records and reporting.

BUSINESS ADVANTAGE

What a good fit can improve

A more consistent journey for clients and a clearer daily view for the team, if settings match how the business operates.

PROOF OF FIT

How to check before committing

Walk through a test booking from first enquiry to rebooking. Check changes, cancellations, no-shows, staff permissions and data export before migrating.

PRACTICAL SELECTION CHECKLIST

Questions to ask before you decide.

  • Map the full booking journey, including phone, walk-in, online and repeat appointments.
  • Check service durations, buffers, rooms, chairs, staff skills and multi-location needs.
  • Review deposit, cancellation, reminder and consent settings against your policies.
  • Ask what happens to client records and booking history if you leave the platform.
  • Test on both a phone and a computer with a realistic example week.
  • Plan training and a rollback route before moving live bookings.
Keep the decision measurable. Record what matters today, what needs to work better, the full cost and who will own the system. Compare providers against the same checklist, then test a realistic workflow before rollout.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Do you only work with clinics?

No. Risenup supports clinics, salons, barbers, beauty and tattoo studios, allied health and other service businesses. The Margin Tracker is a separate workbook designed for clinics.

Do I need to replace my existing software?

No. We first review the systems you already use. If they meet your needs, we can focus on better setup, clearer routines or supported integrations. Replacement is considered only when there is a clear reason.

What happens on the first call?

We discuss what you want to improve, how the work happens today and what tools are involved. If there is a fit, we outline a proposed scope and fee for you to review before any project begins.

Can you guarantee a margin increase or time saving?

No. We can agree a baseline and measure a change, but outcomes depend on your circumstances, the quality of available data, implementation and team adoption.

Do you provide accounting, legal or employment advice?

No. Risenup helps organise operational information and systems. Engage a qualified accountant, bookkeeper, lawyer or employment adviser for professional advice in their field.

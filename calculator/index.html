CLEAR ASSUMPTIONS

How to use the estimate well.

A capacity scenario helps you ask better questions. It cannot tell you whether customers will book, whether your team can deliver more or whether a change is worth its cost. Validate the details before acting.

What does this calculator estimate?

It uses your own service sales per delivered hour to create a what-if scenario: the additional sales and, if you enter variable costs, contribution that may be possible if you use some spare appointment capacity. It does not claim this amount is currently being lost or guaranteed.

Which businesses can use it?

It is designed for appointment-led service businesses such as salons, barbers, beauty and tattoo studios, allied health practices and clinics. Use service sales excluding GST where possible. For multiple locations or distinct service types, run separate estimates where you have the figures.

Where do I find the figures?

Use service sales from your accounting or booking system, excluding GST and ideally retail sales. For bookable hours, add the hours your team and service spaces were genuinely available for client work in a typical week. Delivered hours are the time spent providing paid services. Estimates are okay if you treat them as estimates.

Is the contribution estimate net profit?

No. It subtracts only the variable-cost percentage you provide. It excludes wages, rent, fixed overheads, tax, owner drawings and the cost of generating demand.

Does this calculator store or send my figures?

No. The calculator runs in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to Risenup or saved by this page. If you contact us separately, only information you include in your enquiry is submitted.

How should I use the result?

Treat it as a planning prompt. Check customer demand, cancellations, service mix, prices, team rosters and direct costs. A scenario becomes useful only when you test its assumptions against your actual operating data.

A PRACTICAL RESEARCH ROUTINE

Make external data
useful to you.

Market information varies by service type, location, customer mix and business size. Record the source and date, avoid comparing unlike businesses and use external statistics as context rather than as a target or promise.

01

Define the question

Are you assessing local demand, service pricing, competitor offers, staffing costs or a potential location?

02

Choose a relevant source

Check the source’s coverage, methodology, geography and reference dates before using a figure.

03

Compare with your records

Review your own enquiries, bookings, repeat visits, service mix, costs and capacity where appropriate.

04

Test a small decision

Choose one change, set a baseline and review what happens before expanding the decision.

Why there are no average margin claims here: a national business count or broad industry statistic is not a dependable substitute for a representative, service-specific margin dataset. Risenup does not publish benchmark ranges without a clear, verifiable sample and methodology.

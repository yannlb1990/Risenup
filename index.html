A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

What kinds of business problems can you help with?

We start with the work you want to improve: unclear service margins, repeated admin, disconnected systems, slow hand-offs, limited reporting or a process that depends too much on the owner. The first step is to understand the workflow and decide whether consulting, a custom tool, automation or integration is the right fit.

Can you build an AI tool and connect it to my current software?

Yes, when the use case and systems support a practical connection. We map the workflow, check data access and provider capabilities, then agree the tool’s scope, human review, running cost and maintenance before building. You keep a clear handover and know what the tool can and cannot do.

What will I receive from a consultation?

We first clarify your goals, current systems and the day-to-day work causing friction. If there is a fit, you receive an agreed scope, deliverables, measures and fee before implementation starts.

How do you show whether the solution helped?

We agree a baseline and a measure before the work starts, such as repeat data entry, time spent on a defined task, error hand-offs or how quickly a report can be prepared. We review the result with you; we do not promise a specific saving before measuring your process.

What is the Margin Tracker?

A separate self-serve Excel and Google Sheets workbook for Australian clinic owners. It helps you organise and explore clinic figures; see its product page for the current workbook details and purchase terms.

Will the Margin Tracker replace my booking or accounting software?

No. It is a workbook for organising and exploring clinic figures. It does not replace your accounting, booking or payroll platform.

Do you provide accounting, legal, HR or cybersecurity advice?

No. Risenup helps organise clinic figures and operating systems, but that is not a substitute for a qualified accountant, bookkeeper, lawyer, HR adviser or cyber specialist.

How do I get started?

Book a discovery call for consulting or email hello@risen-up.com with a general question. You can also review the Margin Tracker for a self-serve starting point.

Do you provide marketing services?

No. Risenup focuses on margin integrity and operational engineering: clinic numbers, costs, resource allocation, forecasting and customer retention systems.

Can you guarantee more profit, bookings or time saved?

No. Outcomes depend on the business, data quality, capacity and implementation. Any future opportunity estimate would be a scenario for exploring assumptions, not a forecast or promise.
